What it does

01

Transcribes

Captures mic and system audio separately. Every word, every speaker, timestamped.

02

Apple Intelligence generates a summary on your device. Never leaves your Mac.

03

Remembers

Every meeting saved locally. Search, read, export whenever you need it.

04

Stays quiet

Sits in the background. Pause, resume, stop. No interruptions.
