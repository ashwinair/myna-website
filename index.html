Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for focus. Myna captures everything so you don't have to.

🎙️
Live Transcription

Real-time voice-to-text from your microphone and system audio simultaneously. Every word captured as it's spoken.

✨
AI Summaries

One tap to generate a clean, structured summary of your entire meeting using Apple Intelligence — right on your Mac.

📅
Calendar-Aware

Myna reads your calendar and surfaces upcoming meetings automatically. Know what's next before it starts.

🔒
100% On-Device

No cloud processing. No servers. Your transcripts and summaries are generated and stored entirely on your Mac.

📋
Copy & Export

Copy your transcript or summary with a single click. Share it anywhere — Notion, email, Slack, or your own notes.

🖥️
Truly Native Mac

Built with SwiftUI for macOS. Feels at home in your dock. Fast, lightweight, and designed for how you actually work.

How it works

From calendar to notes
in three steps.

1
Connect your calendar

Myna reads your macOS Calendar to detect upcoming meetings automatically. No setup needed — just grant access once.

2
Record & transcribe

Hit record when your meeting starts. Myna captures mic and system audio in real-time and transcribes every word live.

3
Get your summary

When the meeting ends, Myna generates a clean AI summary using Apple Intelligence. Your notes are ready in seconds.

Privacy

Your conversations
stay yours.

We didn't build a server because we didn't need to. Myna runs entirely on your Mac.

🛡️

No cloud, ever

Your audio, transcripts, and summaries never leave your device. There's no backend to breach.

🤖

On-device AI

Summaries are generated by Apple Intelligence, running locally on Apple Silicon. Fast, private, free.

📵

Works offline

No internet required for transcription or summaries. Your notes are available even on a plane.
