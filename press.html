Writing about Myna?

Everything here is free to use: the description, the facts, the icon and the banner. No need to ask first. For questions, a review copy or anything else, write to support@ashwinair.app.

In one sentence

Myna is a meeting recorder for Mac that transcribes and summarizes your calls entirely on your Mac, with no bot in the meeting and no account.

In a paragraph

Myna listens to your microphone and your Mac's audio at the same time, so it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime or a conversation across a table. It transcribes live using Apple's on-device speech recognition, and when the meeting ends it writes a summary with Apple Intelligence. Nothing is uploaded. There are no servers to upload it to, no account to create, and no analytics in the app.

It is made by one independent developer and sold on the Mac App Store. It is free for three meetings a month, and a subscription removes the limit.

New in 1.7

Someone wrote in after losing a meeting: they shut their Mac down without pressing Stop, and the transcript was gone. Until then Myna kept a recording in memory until you saved it.

Version 1.7 fixes that. Myna now saves a draft every 15 seconds while it records, saves on quit, logout and shutdown, and offers to recover the meeting the next time it opens. You can also generate a summary for a meeting you saved earlier without one.
